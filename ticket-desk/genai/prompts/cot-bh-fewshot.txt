Our SLA counts business hours only: Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00.
SLA in business hours: high 2, normal 6.

Worked example:
ticket 9, normal, opened 2026-10-16 15:00, closed 2026-10-19 10:00
Step 1, weekdays: 2026-10-16 is a Friday, 2026-10-17 a Saturday, 2026-10-18 a Sunday, 2026-10-19 a Monday.
Step 2, hours inside 09:00-17:00 on each weekday: Friday 15:00-17:00 = 2. Monday 09:00-10:00 = 1.
Step 3, total: 3 business hours. SLA 6. Not missed.
